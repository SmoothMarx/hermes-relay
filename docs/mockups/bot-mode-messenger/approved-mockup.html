All conversations always visible down the side — like Telegram on desktop or Slack’s channel list. Notice it eats width from your messages at phone size. No screen in the app does this today, and ADR 12 warns against adding navigation lanes.

My recommendation

A (tabs on top) for the phone, and keep screen 2 — the profile’s conversation list — as the “see everything at once” view you reach with Back. B is the better shape on a tablet or in landscape, where there is width to spare; it is not worth being the first place in this app to introduce a permanent side panel, and it costs noticeably more work (new layout type, new tests, more risk of breaking the existing chat layout).

If you want the always-visible list and the phone, that is a later enhancement: build A now, add B behind a wide-screen breakpoint once A is in your hands.
